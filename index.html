Kana Practice – learn hiragana & katakana

A calm, free practice app for the Japanese kana. Pick exactly the characters you're studying – single kana, whole rows, or every row up to the one you've reached – then practice just those. There's no timer and no sign-up.

Four ways to practice

  • Flashcards – flip each card and say whether you knew it.
  • Multiple choice – pick the right reading (or the right kana) from four options.
  • Type it – see a kana and type its romaji; Hepburn and other spellings (shi / si) are accepted.
  • Write it – see the sound and draw the kana with a mouse, finger or stylus. Every stroke is checked for shape, direction and order, with an animated stroke-order demo when you need it.

What's covered

All 46 basic hiragana and katakana, voiced kana with dakuten and handakuten (が, ぱ…), and combination kana (きゃ, しゅ, ちょ…). Missed characters come back later in the round, progress is tracked per character, and a weak-spots list lets you review what you've recently missed.
